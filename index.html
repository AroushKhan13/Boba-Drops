How To Have The Perfect Day Indoors

Looking for a cozy day indoors?

The Satin Sisters have you covered!

Make a mug brownie!

  • 3 Tbsp Flour
  • 3 Tbsp Cocoa Powder
  • Pinch Salt
  • 3 Tbsp Milk
  • 2 1/2 Tbsp Oil
  • Handful of Chocolate Chips
  1. Combine flour, cocoa powder, and salt
  2. Whisk in oil and milk
  3. Fold in chocolate chips
